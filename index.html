Common questions

Before you book

Can I claim my treatment on insurance?

If your health policy covers dental treatment, we give you itemised bills, X-rays and reports for your claim. Our front desk helps you fill the forms.

What if I'm nervous about the dentist?

Tell us when you book. We offer longer appointments, numbing gel before every injection and a "stop" hand signal that we always respect.

Do you see dental emergencies?

Yes. We keep emergency slots every day, including Sunday morning. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll fit you in.

Is there parking?

Free parking for cars and two-wheelers in the building basement. The clinic is a two-minute walk from Athwa Gate.
